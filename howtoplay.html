How to Play Wavelength

Welcome to Wavelength!

  1. Tap the board to reveal the scale and the target. Only the clue giver should look at this screen.
  2. Give a clue that suggests where the target lies between the two extremes.
  3. The clue giver can optionally rate the pair's difficulty and fun below the scale. Each choice saves automatically; you can change it before passing the device.
  4. Click “Hide for Guessers”. The target and feedback card disappear. Pass the device to the guessing team.
  5. The guessing team discusses the clue, then drags the needle to its chosen position.
  6. Click “Reveal Target” to award points: 5 in the center, 3 in the next band, 1 in the outer band, and 0 outside the target.
  7. Click “Next Round” to switch teams. Only the next clue giver should look at the revealed target.

O feedback é opcional. Se não houver conexão, as escolhas ficam pendentes no navegador para envio posterior. A avaliação refere-se ao par de extremos, não à dica ou ao desempenho da equipe.
